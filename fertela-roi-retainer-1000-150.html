How the defaults are set
Cost per enquiry
Blended across Google and Meta, from keyword research and platform experience in your target markets. Actual costs are measured and reported from the first month.
Booking rate
Share of enquiries that book a first consultation, by video or in person. Assumes your coordinators follow up promptly.
Attendance rate
Share of booked consultations that are attended.
Treatment rate
Share of attended consultations that go on to treatment, in line with commonly cited industry figures of about one in three.
Average treatment value
For example, one donor egg cycle. Change it to match your own treatment mix.
Qualified consultations
Every attended consultation is assumed to meet the qualification criteria agreed with you before launch.
Costs
"Ad spend only" counts what you pay Google and Meta directly. "Ad spend + Fertela fees" adds €1,000 a month and €150 per qualified attended consultation.
Return
Treatment revenue generated for each euro of cost. With ad spend only, this is your return on ad spend.
