Who we are

A Barcelona-based, internationally focused patient-acquisition agency for private fertility clinics across Spain. We build the corridor between your clinic and the international patients already looking for it.

How we partner

A small number of clinic partnerships at a time: one clinic partner per international corridor. No percentage of your ad spend, so no incentive to inflate it. And we measure what your clinic actually banks: consultations that happen, not leads delivered.

Our principles

Fertility care is one of the most emotionally complex, financially significant decisions in medicine. We market accordingly: honest claims, no pressure, no trading on hope, and patient privacy treated as a design requirement.

How we work

Three principles behind every corridor we build.

Built in the source market

Campaigns researched, written, and run in the patient's home market and language, built around the questions international patients actually ask.

Your accounts, your data

Campaigns run in your ad accounts; enquiries land in your systems. Patient data stays inside the clinic. Fertela never needs to hold it.

Compliance as architecture

GDPR Article 9, Spanish publicidad sanitaria rules, and source-market ad codes are constraints we design around, not fix later.

Why fertility marketing is different

Fundamentally distinct from generic healthcare marketing. Six realities we build around:

Long consideration cycles

International patients are often years into their fertility journey, and months into comparing clinics abroad, by the time a first enquiry reaches you. Campaigns built for instant conversion misread the decision process.

The multi-cycle reality

Treatment often takes more than one cycle. Honest communication respects that journey from the beginning.

Success rates, handled with care

Success rates are easy to misuse and widely misunderstood. We treat them the way patients and regulators deserve: precisely, in context, or not at all.

Regulated channels

Health advertising rules meet platform limits: Google and Meta both restrict how fertility audiences can be reached. We design within the rules, not around them.

Diverse patient journeys

International patients are far more than heterosexual couples: solo mothers by choice, same-sex couples, patients priced or aged out of their home systems. Each path shapes the message we write.

The cross-border layer

Language, cost transparency, travel logistics, and legal differences between home and destination shape every decision. The corridor is the product.
